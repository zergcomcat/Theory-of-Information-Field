# Theory of Information Field (TIF)
## Axiomatic Quantum-Information Ontology and Systems Analysis of Reality

The fundamental framework of this model is built upon a continuous, scientifically legitimate triad:  
**Information → Logic → Mathematics**

This model does not rewrite the existing equations of quantum mechanics or General Relativity (GR). Instead, it translates their physical meaning from the metaphorical language of "material objects" into a rigorous, isomorphic language of systems theory, quantum informatics, and data conservation laws. Physical space, time, and constants are recognized here not as fundamental substances, but as secondary interface effects.

## Architectural Layers of Reality

### 1. Fundamental Layer: Quantum-Information Basis (Layer 0)
*   **Pure Potential of Information (The Field):** A dimensionless abstract capacity containing neither matter nor space, serving as the source of all possible distinctions.
*   **Superposition (True NULL State):** A state of absolute semantic emptiness of a cell prior to interaction. Hidden variables do not exist; physical variables (mass, charge, coordinates) are fundamentally absent from the system's memory until the measurement procedure is invoked.
*   **Quantum Measurement (Act of Rendering):** A request made by an observer or an instrument that instantly transitions the system from a potential state to an actual one.

### 2. Logical Layer: Causality Principle and Wave Duality
*   **Discrete Spacetime Track (Append-Only WORM Log):** The dynamics of any changes are governed by the strict *Write Once, Read Many* (WORM) hardware rule. The past is hardware-locked with a *Read Only* flag. The accumulation of these immutable micro-singularities forms the arrow of time and entropy.
*   **Wave Mode (Lazy Evaluation):** The principle of least action. In the absence of measurement, the system economically allocates computational resources by not calculating the exact coordinates of objects, instead describing them as a probability field.
*   **Wave Function Collapse (Hash Crystallization):** The instantaneous transition of the system from wave mode to corpuscular (discrete) mode at the final point of event fixation during measurement.

### 3. Epistemological Layer: Observer Boundaries
*   **Anthropic Epistemological Horizon (Isolated Guest OS):** The physical limitation of the human mind and instruments, operating as an isolated context that lacks direct root access to the Host core.
*   **Quantum Uncertainty Principle (Computational Blindness):** The fundamental impossibility for a local observer to know the global state of the entire system prior to making a measurement.

### 4. Mathematical Layer: Metric Dynamics and Cosmology
*   **Gravity Effect (Batch Metric Processing):** An interface effect of computational resource economy. Mass is an anomalously high density of the archived WORM data stack. To avoid calculating links with trillions of fixed points individually, the system batch-processes calculations, rendering the trajectory of new events along the path of least computational cost (the "attraction" effect). This renders Dark Matter and Dark Energy hypotheses redundant.
*   **Gravitational Collapse (Critical I/O Deadlock):** A point where the transaction log has reached a critical density, causing the computation step to spiral into an infinite loop. The system loses the ability to perform operations in this sector, and the spatial metric "folds," protecting the rest of the database from a cascade failure.
*   **Black Hole Event Horizon (Frozen UI Thread):** A physical boundary at which, for an external observer, the system computation step and the flow of time approach infinite deceleration.
*   **Singularity (Core Kernel Error):** The physical coordinate of a logical algorithm deadlock, temporarily excluded from the global continuous computation cycle.
*   **Hawking Radiation (WORM Log Fixation Process):** A continuous process of registering new acts of information division at the quantum level, occurring at the boundary of the deadlocked sector.
*   **Cosmological Evolution Coefficient (Load Factor):** The mathematical ratio of already occurred events to the total potential of the Universe domain (`v_log / v_max_capacity`), establishing a stable dynamic plateau for all universal constants ($c, h, G$).
